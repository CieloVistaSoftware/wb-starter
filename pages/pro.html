⭐ WB Pro

Showpiece effects and advanced widgets on top of the free, MIT-licensed core.

What Pro adds

confetti, fireworks, snow, sparkle, particle, rainbow, typewriter, countup, parallax, marquee and stagelight.

How it works

The core stays free. WB Pro is a separate pack: load it after wb-starter and its behaviors work like the built-in ones. Free markup behaves the same with or without it.
