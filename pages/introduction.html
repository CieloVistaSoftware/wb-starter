Introduction to wb-starter

A zero-build website framework. You write HTML5. That is the whole API.

What it is

wb-starter adds behavior to the HTML you already write. A behavior attaches either to a semantic element itself (an <article> becomes a card, a <dialog> a modal, a <details> an accordion) or to any element through an x-* attribute (x-ripple, x-tooltip, x-draggable). There is no build step, no bundler, no JSX and no custom-element vocabulary, and everything renders in the light DOM, where your CSS and your devtools reach it. 

<article x-draggable x-ripple>
  <h3>Hello</h3>
  <p>A card you can drag, with a ripple on press.</p>
</article>

The <article> is still an <article>. Inspect it and you see the element you wrote, with classes on it: HTML5 in, HTML5 out.

Why it ends the traditional framework

Every popular framework makes the same trade: to get components, you give up HTML. React turns markup into JSX and a build pipeline, Vue and Angular add template languages and compilers, and Web Components hide your markup behind shadow roots your CSS cannot reach. wb-starter makes the opposite trade. The browser already has the components; wb-starter adds what they are missing. Here is what a team pays for with each, and where to see it for yourself.
